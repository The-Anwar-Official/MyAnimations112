Chai · A Brewing Ritual
Step 1 / 5
1

Heat the Milk

Pour 1 cup of water and 2 cups of milk into a saucepan. Warm over a medium flame — don't let it come to a full boil. Gentle heat lets the chai build a creamy base.

💡Stir occasionally so the milk doesn't catch at the bottom of the pot.
2

Add the Tea Leaves

Scatter 2 teaspoons of tea leaves into the warm milk-water. Bring it back to a gentle simmer. As the leaves steep, the colour slowly deepens — that's the soul of the chai waking up.

💡Good-quality leaves make an enormous difference. A pinch of CTC gives a strong, malty body.
3

Sugar & Spices

Add 2–3 teaspoons of sugar to taste and stir gently. For a warm, peppery lift, drop in crushed ginger, cardamom pods, or cloves.

💡Stir in circles so the sugar dissolves evenly — don't scrape the bottom too hard or the chai turns bitter.
4

Boil & Strain

Let the chai boil for 3–4 minutes. When it turns a deep brown and perfumes the air, take it off the heat. Strain through a sieve into a cup, pressing the leaves gently to release every drop.

💡Over-boiling makes chai bitter. Lift the pot right at the "rolling" stage for a smooth cup.
5

Serve & Enjoy

Your steaming cup of Indian chai is ready. Pour it hot into cups and share it. Pair with biscuits, namkeen, or pakoras for the perfect chai-time ritual.

💡Chai always tastes best when shared — sip it with family or friends.
